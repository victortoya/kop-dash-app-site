Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restock a shelf?

Tap an empty or low shelf to restock it instantly. Once you hire a Clerk (Pramuniaga), shelves are restocked automatically.

How does the cashier work?

When a customer reaches the counter, tap the cashier once to scan their items, then tap again to collect the money. After hiring a Cashier (Kasir), scanning becomes fully automatic.

What is Rush Hour (Dash)?

Serve customers to fill the Dash meter, then tap DASH to trigger a 30-second Rush Hour: visitors ×3 and prices ×1.5. It has a 10-minute cooldown.

Can I earn while the app is closed?

Yes. Your store earns offline income at 50 % of the normal rate. The cap starts at 2 hours and increases up to 12 hours by upgrading the Manager (Manajer).

How do I unlock a new branch?

Reach ★3 reputation in the current branch, then pay the unlock cost shown on the Branch Map. Branches open in order: Jungle → Fishpond → Graveyard → Isekai → Moon.

What are Co-op Points (CP)?

Co-op Points are earned by gaining stars and maxing out shelves. Spend them on the Member Card to unlock perks that apply to every branch, such as higher sale prices and faster scan times.

What is the Visitor Album?

The Album tracks the 14 unique visitor types you've served. Reaching Bronze, Silver and Gold milestones for each visitor unlocks a permanent tip bonus for that type.

What is Kliwon Night?

Every Thursday night, spirit visitors (Pocong and Kuntilanak) visit the KopDash Graveyard twice as often. The event is marked with a banner in-game.

My progress has disappeared. What can I do?

Save data is stored locally on your device. If you have iCloud enabled in Settings, your progress is backed up automatically. Contact us at toya.luckyrich@gmail.com and we will do our best to help.

Does KopDash require an internet connection?

No. KopDash plays fully offline. An internet connection is only needed to load rewarded ads (optional) and to sync iCloud saves.

How do I reset my game?

At this time there is no in-app reset option. If you need to start over, delete and reinstall the app. Note that this will permanently erase your local save data.
