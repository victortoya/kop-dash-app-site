Privacy Policy

Overview

KopDash Co-op ("we", "us", "the app") is a pixel art store management game for iPhone. This policy explains what data is collected when you play KopDash, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

KopDash does not require an account, does not collect your name or email address, and does not sell personal data to third parties.

Data We Collect

Game Save Data

Your game progress (Rupiah, branch state, shelf levels, staff, album, settings) is saved locally on your device as an encrypted JSON file. If you enable iCloud in your device settings, the save file is also stored in your personal iCloud account. We never access or transmit your save data to our servers.

Anonymous Usage Analytics

We collect anonymous, aggregated events to understand how the game is played and to fix bugs. Examples of events logged:

  • Session start and end
  • Tutorial step reached
  • Shelf upgrade and unlock
  • Branch unlock
  • Dash (Rush Hour) activated
  • Offline income claimed
  • Mission completed
  • Rewarded ad viewed

These events contain no personal identifiers. They cannot be linked back to you.

Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

KopDash shows optional rewarded ads powered by Google AdMob. Watching a rewarded ad is always voluntary — you are never required to watch an ad to play.

Before AdMob loads any ad, the app presents a consent form required by the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Depending on your choice:

  • If you consent — AdMob may use your Advertising ID (IDFA) to serve personalised ads. Apple will first ask for permission via the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt.
  • If you decline or opt out — AdMob serves non-personalised ads only. No IDFA is accessed.

Google's privacy policy applies to data collected by AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy

How We Use Data

  • To save and restore your game progress on your device and iCloud
  • To understand gameplay patterns and improve the game
  • To serve optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob

We do not use any data for marketing, profiling, or automated decision-making.

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob

Ad network used for optional rewarded ads. May collect device identifiers for ad personalisation (with your consent via ATT).

Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Apple iCloud

If you enable iCloud sync, your save file is stored in your own iCloud account under Apple's standard data protection. We have no access to your iCloud data.

Privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy

Children's Privacy

KopDash is rated 4+ on the App Store. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. We do not display interest-based advertising to users who have indicated they are under the age of consent in their region.

If a parent or guardian believes their child has provided personal data, please contact us at toya.luckyrich@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

Data Retention & Deletion

Local save data is deleted when you uninstall the app.

iCloud save data is deleted when you remove the app from iCloud Drive or delete your iCloud account.

Anonymous analytics are retained in aggregated form and cannot be individually deleted because they are not linked to any identifier.

To request deletion of any data we may hold, contact us at toya.luckyrich@gmail.com.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable privacy law (such as GDPR or CCPA) including the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you.

Because KopDash collects no account information, most requests can be fulfilled simply by uninstalling the app. For any other requests, contact us at toya.luckyrich@gmail.com.

You can withdraw your ATT consent at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
